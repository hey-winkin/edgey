## Hosted staging evidence (21 November 2026)

`edgey-staging` runs on Vercel Pro from [GitHub `main`](https://github.com/hey-winkin/edgey/tree/main), on Node `24.x` pinned by `engines.node`. It uses the committed `vercel.json` and native one-minute Vercel Cron. All Deployments protection is on. Automatic deploys from `main` are enabled, but the first automatic deploy has not yet been observed. Pull request previews are disabled. The apex `edgey.site` and `www.edgey.site` are reserved for the live shop.

The Supabase project `edgey` in Stockholm (`eu-north-1`) has all 36 migrations from the tested commit and 11 published fixture variants, loaded through SQL rather than the guarded importer. Supabase Auth uses custom SMTP through Resend with `orders@mail.edgey.site` on the verified `mail.edgey.site` domain (DMARC `p=none`). One staff account has an approved TOTP factor; the temporary test identity was removed.

- **Not run or unproven:** Stripe payment, signed webhook and paid return, wallets, invoices, a paid order through picking and dispatch, and last-blade contention and expiry. The real emailed buyer magic-link callback, staff enquiry reply and the full authenticated staff mobile visual matrix also remain unproven. The Auth email request succeeded; Resend accepted six app notices, which does not prove inbox delivery. The full privacy notice, retention and purge schedule, and hosted abuse controls for public intake are not signed off.
- **Finder timing:** Warm RPC p95 was 154 ms from a runner that was probably in the US, based on routing hints rather than confirmed geolocation. The database is in Stockholm. The network-inclusive sample exceeded 50 ms, but the target remains unproven; remeasure near the database before calling it a performance failure.
- **Open defects:** The privacy notice links to itself and is not adequate for public intake; the enquiry form loses input entered before hydration; quick staff sign-in retries can produce a full-page HTTP 500 (cause not isolated); the product specification arrow drops onto a separate line; and compact dialog actions require scrolling. Fixes are in progress for a separate pull request.

## Owner decisions before opening up (PRD §8)

- **Catalogue:** Approve supplier fit and evidence for each published variant and alias, confirm its physical pick label and enter actual opening stock. Leave unverified variants in draft.
- **Commercial and Finance:** Approve the price matrix, all-units discounts and margin after delivery, VAT status and invoice details, customer terms and returns. Check both a card PDF and a Net 30 PDF in Stripe's sandbox against the saved amounts. Card passes VAT-inclusive amounts with a text breakdown; Net 30 passes explicit line tax amounts. Neither a matching total nor a test invoice establishes statutory compliance.
- **Fulfilment:** Confirm the carrier, cut-off, coverage and services by postcode, holiday calendar, packaging and sample eligibility. Have a qualified adviser or Trading Standards confirm any remote-sale, age and delivery checks for the actual products and destinations before offering them.
- **Operations:** Name the people who pick, answer enquiries, approve samples and handle failed or returned orders. Supply the official business name, approved email and address for the contact page. Publish the full privacy notice, including who controls the data, purpose, sharing, retention and how to exercise rights. Set the approved notice URL, agree the retention and redaction rules for intake, Finder events, approved trials and applications, orders and unconfirmed Auth accounts, then schedule and monitor the purge before accepting public submissions.
- **Finance (credit):** Approve initial limits, approval and suspension rules, ownership of overdue invoices and how to reconcile them. Run the full 30-day invoice lifecycle in staging before enabling Net 30.

Use the [design acceptance pass](../design.md#visual-acceptance-pass) to review extension screens in both themes before the first live release.
Repeat the compact keyboard and text-resize checks on deployed staging. Check a screen reader, touch controls on a physical phone, and an A4 pick list from the warehouse printer. Automated Chromium checks don't establish those results.

## Acceptance evidence

- `pnpm test:acceptance` covers the card, trial and Net 30 acceptance scenarios in PRD §7 and the §1 weekend journey. Net 30 runs the concurrent checkout and timeout cases against local Supabase, then drives finance approval, checkout, dispatch, partial payment and settlement through the production build in Chromium. All provider responses are simulated; webhook verification uses signed request bytes. The visual checklist uses separate Chromium captures.
- `pnpm test:e2e` uses axe on representative buyer and staff route states in both themes, with dedicated checks at 1440, 390 and 320 px for enlarged text, compact control targets, keyboard interaction and pick-list print emulation. The route inventory requires new pages to be accounted for. The material-study contrast that axe can't infer is measured separately; other inconclusive results are retained in the `axe-manual-review` CI artifact for manual review.
- The navigation suite also scans the open shop and staff menus at all three widths. It covers keyboard dismissal and focus, non-JavaScript shop links, history navigation, staff roles and sign-out, and separate shop and staff 404 shells.
- Deliberate departures from the visual reference: the product card is named by its part code and product name, with its match, fit, stock and prices as the accessible description. The quick view uses the product page’s themed drawing or neutral part-code plate; it links to the approved photograph when available. The centred wire mark opens the menu; the theme switch uses icons, and the Basket button shows the count with a visible label on wider screens. Staff materials use labelled checkboxes instead of a multi-select. The design pack itself is unchanged.
- LUK-75 adds a storefront-only exception to the neutral masthead in PRD §§4.2 and 4.5: Glacier and Citron samples drift inside the stationary wire mark. The shop menu can pause the motion, and the fill stays static when reduced motion is requested. The smaller staff mark stays neutral.
- The staff requests desk shows enquiry reply and trial message state. “Sent” means the provider accepted the message, not that it arrived. The contact and privacy pages remain informational until approved content is supplied. Keep `EDGEY_INDEXING_ENABLED=false` and `EDGEY_FINDER_ATTRIBUTION_ENABLED=false` in staging; hosted attribution also requires an approved notice and buyer opt-in.
- The commercial report uses retained data, not a complete customer cohort. An exact search with no attributed checkout or a dispatched trial with no confirmed purchase link is not proof there was no purchase. Exact-match conversion covers only qualifying links from consenting browsers; the report still counts all exact searches.
- Repeat the [hosted Finder RPC measurement](staging.md#6-run-the-weekend-staging-checklist-on-the-deployed-site) from near the database. The audit baseline passed the 50 ms warm-RPC target locally; the mobile-emulated figure is a separate browser metric, not physical-device evidence.
